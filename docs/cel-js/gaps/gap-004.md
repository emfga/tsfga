# GAP-004 — catastrophic backtracking

**Direction: availability.** RE2 is linear in the subject's
length by construction. A JavaScript `RegExp` backtracks, and
`^(a+)+$` is exponential.

Measured on three runtimes — see
[`../regex/backtracking.md`](../regex/backtracking.md) for the
curves, the methodology and the re-runnable script.

The finding that matters is not the numbers but their spread:
**JavaScriptCore bounds backtracking and V8 does not.** Bun
plateaus near 420 ms at any subject length; Node and Deno reach
20.9 s and 22.6 s at 32 characters and keep climbing. An earlier
revision measured this on Bun alone and concluded it was bounded.

Three controls were measured and all three failed: a length cap
(the blow-up starts below any usable limit), a wall-clock timeout
(nondeterministic authorization), and the evaluation cost budget
(prices the pattern at 7 against a limit of 100 — it bounds
context-driven work, not backtracking).

**Closed in tsfga by dropping `matches()`.** There is no reachable
input because there is no call.
